## 1. Emotional Philosophy

The goal of *Until Sunrise* is to deliver **authentic emotional truth** rather than emotional manipulation.

- **No Artificial Drama:** Never use cheap tragedy, exaggerated arguments, or artificial twists to force tears.
- **Resonance Through Truth:** Emotional power comes from quiet recognition—when the player sees real human habits, real regrets, and real moments of care.

---

## 2. Core Emotional Spectrum

| Emotion | Source Manifestation |
| --- | --- |
| **Quiet Warmth** | The cozy safety of a late-night call, staying online together while doing separate things, a familiar joke. |
| **Nostalgia** | Looking back at early memories (2022 first meeting through Hina, early messages) with fondness and gentle sadness. |
| **Regret & Reflection** | Dũng realizing what he took for granted: her holding on, her lowering her ego, her capacity to forgive. |
| **Hurt & Vulnerability** | Ngọc Anh feeling left out when Dũng acts alone or walks away during conflict; Dũng feeling overwhelmed by sharp words. |
| **Gratitude & Forgiveness** | Recognizing that staying present, listening, and forgiving require immense courage. |
| **Healing (Until Sunrise)** | Reaching a state of peace, mutual understanding, and maturity as morning light breaks. |

---

## 3. Emotional Safety & Respect

- **No Villainization:** Both characters must be treated with deep empathy.
- **Respect for Memory:** Lived experiences are presented as preserved moments of learning and gratitude.
