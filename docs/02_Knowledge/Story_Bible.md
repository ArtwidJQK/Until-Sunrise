## Story Premise & Mission

*Until Sunrise* is an interactive cinematic memory experience about two ordinary people—Dũng and Ngọc Anh—learning how to love with deeper understanding, communication, patience, and care after repeated cycles of hurt and distance.

The experience is NOT created to glorify one person or prove how much someone loved. It exists so that anyone who has ever loved, misunderstood, been hurt, and grown can recognize themselves in these ordinary moments.

---

## Narrative Movements

| Movement | Description & Source Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| **I. Unspoken Baggage** | Both enter the relationship with past wounds, defense mechanisms, and uncommunicated emotional needs. |
| **II. Language Barrier** | Love is present, but expressed in different languages: Dũng seeks calm presence and lightheartedness; Ngọc Anh seeks emotional safety, active listening, and reassurance. |
| **III. The Defensive Loop** | When hurt occurs, Dũng retreats and acts alone; Ngọc Anh holds on, reopens past wounds, and speaks in anger. |
| **IV. The Realization** | Realizing that love alone is insufficient without the specific form of care the partner actually needs. Dũng takes for granted Ngọc Anh's forgiveness, lowering of ego, and willingness to fight for them. |
| **V. Standing on the Same Side** | Shifting from proving who is right to standing together against the problem. Learning to listen without fleeing. |
| **VI. Ordinary Sacredness** | Discovering that small routines—late-night calls, playing online games together, sending small gifts, checking in—are where affection truly lives. |
| **VII. Reflective Acceptance** | Reaching quiet reflection, gratitude, and emotional maturity within the 2026 present-tense journey. |

## Core Narrative Regrets & Realizations (SRC-001)

### Things Dũng Took for Granted:
1. **Her Holding On (Sự Níu Kéo):** Expecting Ngọc Anh to always hold on even when he walked away.
2. **Her Lowering Her Ego (Sự Hạ Cái Tôi):** Failing to realize how much courage it took for her to reach out first after conflict.
3. **Her Capacity to Forgive (Sự Bao Dung):** Taking her forgiveness for granted after repeated separations.

### Dũng's Core Realization:
Love is not just feeling affection; love is staying present when it is difficult, listening when your partner is hurt, and comforting them instead of fleeing to protect yourself.

## Narrative Scope & Boundaries (FD-0002)

- **Approved Narrative Scope:** Up to the **2026 storyline only**.
- **Open-Ended Storyline:** 2026 is an open-ended present moment, NOT a definitive series ending.
- **Forbidden Practices:**
  - DO NOT invent manufactured drama, tragic twists, or villain arcs.
  - DO NOT rewrite the timeline or invent future events beyond 2026.
  - DO NOT reduce real-life emotional memory to game mechanics or points.
