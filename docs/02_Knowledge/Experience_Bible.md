## 1. Experience Identity

*Until Sunrise* is an **Interactive Cinematic Memory Experience**.

- **Not Played to Win:** The player does not earn points, complete quests, manage health bars, or defeat opponents.
- **Experienced to Understand:** The measure of success is what the player notices, understands, and feels regarding human connection, care, and growth.

---

## 2. Player Role: Memory Observer

The player participates as a **Memory Observer**:
- The player enters preserved memories without replacing either Dũng or Ngọc Anh.
- The player observes, interacts with meaningful memory traces, and reflects on emotional choices.
- The player cannot rewrite history, judge either person, or manufacture fake outcomes.

---

## 3. Interaction Philosophy

- **Interaction is Not Gameplay:** Actions (clicking an object, picking up a phone, tuning audio, pausing on a photo) represent the feeling of *"I am touching a part of their lives."*
- **Tactile & Soft:** Interfaces must feel integrated into the environment (e.g., an opening room interface rather than a cold software menu).
- **Reflective Choices:** Choices affect perspective and emotional understanding rather than altering historical canon.

---

## 4. Pacing & Purity

- **Visual & Audio Silence:** Ample room for the player to pause, breathe, and absorb the scene.
- **No Game HUD:** No minimaps, floating quest markers, reward popups, or gamified mechanics.
- **Comfortable Rhythm:** Unhurried scene transitions that feel like remembering or waking up.
