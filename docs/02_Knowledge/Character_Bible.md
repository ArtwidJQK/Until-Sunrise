## 1. Dũng

### Core Identity & Characterization
Dũng reaches for connection openly and warmly. He is vocal with affection, seeks physical and emotional closeness, becomes clingy during quiet moments, remembers small details, and naturally protects Ngọc Anh during shared activities like online gaming.

### Character Flaws (SRC-002)
- **Easily Gives Up:** When conflict escalates, his primary defense mechanism is to withdraw or leave rather than stay and resolve it.
- **Unable to Comfort:** In moments when Ngọc Anh needs emotional soothing, he focuses on trying to quickly end the argument rather than healing her emotional hurt.
- **Choosing Separation First:** In all three relationship phases (2022, 2023, 2026), he is the first person to initiate separation.
- **Impulsive Decision-Making:** Makes quick emotional decisions under stress without anticipating long-term regret.
- **Acting Alone:** Makes decisions affecting both of them unilaterally without asking for her input, unintentionally leaving her feeling excluded.

### Character Growth & Arc
- Moving from self-protective withdrawal to staying present during hard conversations.
- Recognizing the immense value of Ngọc Anh's forgiveness, lowered ego, and willingness to hold on.
- Learning that comforting a partner is more important than winning an argument or escaping discomfort.

---

## 2. Ngọc Anh

### Core Identity & Characterization
Ngọc Anh provides steady, observant care. She notices subtle emotional shifts in Dũng, remembers his preferences, values reassurance, and deeply desires to be a safe emotional harbor where he can share his vulnerabilities.

### Character Flaws (SRC-002)
- **Bringing Up the Past:** During current arguments, she has a habit of reopening past unhealed wounds, making it difficult to resolve the immediate issue.
- **Hurtful Words During Anger:** When overwhelmed by emotional pain, she occasionally uses sharp, hurtful words that do not reflect her true feelings but leave long-lasting wounds on Dũng.

### Character Growth & Arc
- Learning to communicate deep emotional needs directly without resorting to anger or reopening old wounds.
- Balancing her natural impulse to fight for the relationship with building healthy boundaries and seeking mutual emotional safety.
- Expressing vulnerability calmly, allowing Dũng space to learn how to comfort her.

---

## 3. Hina (Supporting Presence)

### Role & Characterization
Hina is the mutual friend through *Liên Quân Mobile* who originally introduced Dũng to Ngọc Anh in 2022, laying the foundation for their connection.

### Authoritative Bounds
- **Gratitude & Respect:** She represents the gentle beginning of their story and must be portrayed with warmth, respect, and sincerity.
- **Strict Boundary:** She must NEVER be used as a dramatic trope or plot device for artificial tragedy. Her presence in memory represents a real person who brought two lives together.
