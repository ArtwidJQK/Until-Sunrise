## Dialogue Principles

1. **Quiet Realism:** Dialogue must sound like real conversation between two young people—casual, understated, gentle, and unforced.
2. **Silence Speaks:** Silence, pauses, and unsaid thoughts carry as much narrative weight as spoken words.
3. **No Manufactured Drama:** Characters must never deliver theatrical speeches, artificial villain monologues, or melodramatic declarations.
4. **Subtle Emotion:** Expressions of affection or hurt are conveyed through small phrases, micro-reactions, and body language.
5. **Language Boundary:** Preserve Vietnamese conversational nuance and authentic tone without introducing modern slang that undermines emotional sincerity.
