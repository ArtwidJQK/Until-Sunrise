## Core Canon Mandates

1. **Canonical Source Authority (FD-0001):**
   All narrative, character, timeline, world, experience, and relationship claims MUST be derived directly from approved sources `SRC-001` (`Until Sunrise.docx`) and `SRC-002` (`Until Sunrise 2.docx`). AI agents SHALL NOT invent, alter, contradict, or reinterpret canon.

2. **Approved Narrative Endpoint (FD-0002):**
   Knowledge documents MUST represent the story up to the approved **2026 storyline only**. The story is open-ended; 2026 is NOT a final franchise ending. Events beyond 2026 remain undefined.

3. **Derivative Knowledge Authority (FD-0003):**
   All generated Knowledge Documents are derivative documentation, NOT independent canon. If a derivative document conflicts with `SRC-001` or `SRC-002`, the source material always prevails.

4. **Conflict & Uncertainty Resolution (FD-0004):**
   If source documents contain ambiguity, contradiction, or missing details:
   - DO NOT invent canon.
   - DO NOT resolve the conflict autonomously.
   - Create or update an entry in `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`.
   - Document the uncertainty and continue work on unrelated tasks.

5. **Minor Creative Expansion Limits (FD-0005):**
   Minor implementation or presentation details MAY be added ONLY when ALL four conditions are met:
   - Source material does not specify the detail;
   - Addition does NOT alter story, characters, relationships, lore, timeline, or narrative intent;
   - Addition exists solely to support technical implementation or visual/audio presentation;
   - Addition remains fully compatible with approved canon.
   *If there is any doubt, treat as an Open Question.*

## Non-Judgmental Storytelling Rule

- Neither character (Dũng or Ngọc Anh) shall be portrayed as an absolute hero, villain, or object of pity.
- All conflicts must be represented with empathy from both perspectives.
- Emotional truth, active listening, and ordinary acts of care must supersede dramatic confrontation or spectacle.
