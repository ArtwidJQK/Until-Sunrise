## 1. World Identity & Atmosphere

The world of *Until Sunrise* is not a grand fantasy setting or a hyper-realistic simulation. It is a **lived-in, cozy, memory-infused environment** where every space feels inhabited by real human presence.

The world should feel:
- **Warm & Safe:** Soft lighting, warm color palettes, and cozy interior spaces.
- **Gentle & Lived-In:** Evidence of human habit rather than sterile perfection (a cup left on a desk, soft light filtering through window curtains, worn paper).
- **Quietly Observant:** The world does not demand attention through spectacle; it invites peaceful contemplation.

---

## 2. Environmental Traces & Presence

Objects and environments carry memory through touch history and repetition:
- **Traces of Use:** A headset resting on a desk, a charging cable, a half-read book, a dented chair cushion.
- **Natural Light & Time:** Golden hour sunlight, quiet evening lamplight, and soft dawn lavenders reflect internal emotional states.
- **Minimalist Focus:** Environments contain only details that carry emotional truth or habit, avoiding visual noise.

---

## 3. Audio Environment & Silence

- **Silence as Audio:** Silence is an active storytelling element, not empty audio space. The quiet after a call ends carries emotional weight.
- **Ambient Soundscapes:** Subtle room tone, distant rain, gentle wind against window glass, soft keyboard taps, or low-wattage lamp hums.
- **Music as Emotion:** Music enters quietly to support internal reflection, never to force dramatic excitement.
