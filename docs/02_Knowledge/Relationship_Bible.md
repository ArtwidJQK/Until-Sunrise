## Core Truth & Relationship Philosophy

The relationship between Dũng and Ngọc Anh is a loving, imperfect bond defined by mutual affection, recurring defensive habits, and gradual growth through shared experience.

Conflict does NOT arise from a lack of love. It arises from how each person responds when feeling hurt or unsafe:
- **Dũng's reaction:** Retreats, acts alone, or chooses separation as self-protection.
- **Ngọc Anh's reaction:** Holds on, brings up past unhealed hurt, and expresses anger through words.

## Love Languages & Expression of Care

### Dũng Expresses Care Through:
1. **Listening:** Giving quiet attention when he feels safe.
2. **Quality Time:** Shared online gaming, long late-night calls, and staying present.
3. **Presence:** Being there consistently during calm periods.
4. **Words of Affection:** Verbal reassurance and warmth when unthreatened.
5. **Playfulness:** Teasing, cute interactions, and lighthearted shared moments.

### Ngọc Anh Expresses Care Through:
1. **Quality Time:** Dedicated time together and shared routines.
2. **Acts of Care:** Noticing small details, remembering preferences, and looking after Dũng.
3. **Forgiveness:** Giving the relationship multiple chances despite past pain.
4. **Lowering Her Ego:** Willingness to compromise and reach out first after distance.
5. **Fighting for the Relationship:** Refusing to let go easily when problems arise.
6. **Emotional Safety & Acceptance:** Wanting to become a safe haven for Dũng's vulnerabilities.

## Mandatory Relationship Rules (SRC-002)

1. **Strict Impartiality:** Until Sunrise does NOT present either character as a villain or a hero. Neither Dũng nor Ngọc Anh is 100% right or 100% wrong.
2. **Dual Perspective:** Every conflict must be understandable from both Dũng's and Ngọc Anh's perspectives.
3. **Empathy Over Judgment:** The player is invited to observe and understand why each person reacts the way they do, never to judge or choose a side.
4. **Respect for Hina:** Hina's memory must be treated with genuine gratitude and respect as the person who first connected them in 2022. She must never be exploited as a dramatic device.
