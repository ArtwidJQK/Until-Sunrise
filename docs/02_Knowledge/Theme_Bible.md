## Core Themes

### 1. Ordinary Acts of Care (Sự Chăm Sóc Bình Dị)
Love in *Until Sunrise* does not live in grand gestures, expensive gifts, or dramatic declarations. It lives in ordinary, daily behavior: staying on a call, checking in, remembering a small preference, playing games online together, and offering quiet presence.

### 2. Standing on the Same Side (Đứng Cùng Một Phía)
The pivotal growth in a relationship occurs when both partners realize that conflict is not an argument to be won against each other, but a shared difficulty to be faced together.

### 3. Growth After Hurt (Trưởng Thành Sau Tổn Thương)
Mistakes and defense mechanisms (withdrawing, choosing separation, speaking in anger, bringing up the past) leave scars, but also become the foundation for deeper emotional awareness, humility, and mature care.

### 4. Active Listening & Presence (Lắng Nghe & Hiện Diện)
Sometimes what a partner needs most is not a quick solution or an escape, but simply an embrace, active listening, and the feeling that their partner will not flee when things get difficult.

### 5. Lowering Ego & Forgiveness (Hạ Cái Tôi & Bao Dung)
Reaching out first after distance, acknowledging one's flaws, and forgiving past hurt require immense strength and vulnerability.
